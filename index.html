The Two Spaces I Work In

Most people who feel stuck are living inside one of two specific tensions. Both are real. Both are workable. But almost no one names them clearly.

I
The System Has You — and You Don't Quite Know It Yet

You're living by a script you didn't consciously choose. What you think you should want, who you think you should be — these were handed to you. By family. By society. By the shape of success everyone around you agreed on.

Something in you resists. But the resistance has no name yet. So it shows up as anxiety, numbness, the feeling that you're doing everything right and something is still deeply wrong.

II
You Feel Free — But You're Not Actually Moving

You've broken from the script. You know what you want — or think you do. And you're still not getting there.

This is the subtler trap. You feel free in your thinking, but something invisible holds the gap open between your vision and your reality. It's not laziness. It's not simple fear. It's something that needs to be seen — not fixed, not pushed through. Seen.

The suffering isn't in the gap itself.
It's in not being able to see it clearly.

— Jessica

How I Work

Clarity work.
Sometimes it touches
something deeper.

I hold a psychology degree from UBC — but the way I work doesn't come from a single framework. I draw from psychology, Western and Eastern philosophy, sociology, history, spirituality, and cultural wisdom depending on what the moment calls for.

I've studied widely and I think sharply. What matters isn't which knowledge system I use — it's whether it illuminates something true for you. Some sessions feel practical. Some feel philosophical. Some touch something spiritual. I follow what's actually happening in the room.

The work can be deeply therapeutic without being therapy. It can be intellectually rigorous without being academic. It can be spiritual without being prescriptive. It is whatever it needs to be.

I don't tell you where to go.
I show you what the map looks like — and you choose.

— Jessica
